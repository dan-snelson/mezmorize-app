## What it is

The Three-minute Memory Method comes from the 1989 Franklin International Institute booklet *Five Secrets to Personal Productivity* by Kurt Hanks and Gerreld L. Pulsipher.

The idea is to review something five times over a year. The total review time is said to be only about three minutes, which is enough to move it from short-term into long-term memory.

Mezmorize applies the method to quotable quotes.
