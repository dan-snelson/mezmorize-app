## Tiles

- The tiles are All, Today, one per stage (Tomorrow, Next Week, Next Month, Next Quarter, Next Year), Completed, Collection Only, Favorites, and Archived.
- Tomorrow lists first reviews due tomorrow. Each other stage tile lists the quotes waiting for that review.
- A first review due today or overdue is in Today. One due later, such as a later day of a file import, is in All until the day before.
- Each row shows the quote and one source line: the author, else the reference, else the title.

## Search and sort

- Search works within the selected tile.
- Sort orders the list.

## Favorites

- Tap the star in a quote's detail to mark it a favorite. It then appears in the Favorites tile.
- On Apple Watch, swipe up or turn the Digital Crown from Today to see Favorites.

## Archive and delete

- Archiving keeps a quote's progress and is reversible.
- Permanent delete is available only from the Archived tile, and it always asks first.

## Share

- The Share button in the quote detail sends the quote, author, and source as plain text through the system share sheet.
- When the source has a web link, tap (click on a Mac) the underlined source to open it in your browser.

> **Note:** Sharing or opening a link never changes the schedule.
