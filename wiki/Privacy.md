- Your quotes stay on your devices and in your private iCloud account.
- Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts, such as how many quotes are reviewed.
- Quote text, authors, sources, and web addresses are never sent.
- The counts are not linked to you and are not used for tracking or advertising.
- Along with the counts, TelemetryDeck receives an anonymous identifier that is not linked to you.
- To turn this off, open Settings (⌘, on a Mac, the … menu → Settings on iPhone and iPad) and turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data under Privacy. Sending stops immediately.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://dan-snelson.github.io/mezmorize-app/privacy.html).
