## Reminders

- Mezmorize can send one notification a day, only on days with quotes to review.
- After you save your first quote it asks for permission.
- Change the time, turn the quote preview off, or turn reminders off in Settings (⌘, on a Mac, the … menu → Settings on iPhone and iPad).
- Tapping a reminder opens Today, and the app icon badge shows how many quotes are due.
- Reminders are local to your device.

## Quote Display

- In Settings → Quote Display you can choose a serif or sans-serif font and make quote text smaller or larger.
- This changes only how an open quote looks.
