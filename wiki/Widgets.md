Add a Mezmorize widget to your Home Screen (iPhone, iPad) or desktop (Mac) to see what is due.

- **Small:** how many quotes are due today. Tap to open Today.
- **Medium and larger:** one quote with its author, and the number due. Tap to open that quote.
- The quote is one that is due today. When none is due, it is a favorite, else any quote. It changes about once an hour.
- Archived quotes never appear.

> **Note:** Widgets only display. They never change your review progress or the schedule.
