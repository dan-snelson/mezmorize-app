Study Mode helps you memorize a quote line by line.

## When it is available

- When the quote is not due: after today's review is done.
- Always, when the Apply memory method switch is off.

## Start

1. Tap the graduation cap at the bottom right of the quote.
2. The quote is laid out one phrase per row (short phrases such as names stay with their neighbor), and a virtual 3x5 index card covers part of it.
3. Touch (click on a Mac) anywhere to move the card over the rows, and drag the grip in its corner to resize it.

## End

- **Mac:** press Esc or ⌘⇧S, click End Study in the toolbar, or click the blurred list.
- **iPhone and iPad:** touch and hold anywhere.
- **iPad:** you can also tap the blurred list.

<!-- -->

- While you study, the reveal button still switches the rows between full text, first letters only, and hidden.

> **Note:** Nothing is saved. Study Mode never changes your review progress or the schedule.
