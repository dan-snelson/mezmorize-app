## Today

- Today shows the quotes due today.
- Missed reviews appear under Overdue, above Today, and are never skipped.

## Mark a quote reviewed

1. Open a quote and read it.
2. Tap Mark Reviewed. The button appears only when the quote is due.
3. Mezmorize moves to the next quote that is due. After the last one, the quote closes (on iPhone, back to the list).

> **Note:** Leaving a quote without marking it reviewed (Back on iPhone, or picking another quote) never changes the schedule.

## The review bar

The review bar at the bottom shows:

- A progress ring
- The review name
- A Due Today or Overdue badge
- The next review date
- Mark Reviewed

The Apply memory method switch is in Edit (the … menu → Edit).

## Self-test with Reveal

- The reveal button cycles the quote between full text, first letters only, and hidden.

> **Note:** Display only. It never changes the schedule.

## Previous and next quote

- **Mac:** use the < > buttons or ⌘[ and ⌘].
- **iPhone and iPad:** swipe left or right.

> **Note:** This never changes the schedule.

## Celebrations

- To play a short celebration animation with each Mark Reviewed, pick an effect in Settings (off by default).
- It is skipped when Reduce Motion is on.
- With a celebration effect picked, an empty Today may also greet you with an animal and a cheerful phrase.
