Support

In-app Help

Mezmorize includes Help that explains the method and how to use the app.

  • Mac: choose Help → Mezmorize Help (⌘?).
  • iPhone and iPad: tap the … menu, then Help.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion is for review only: see Today’s list, open a quote, tap Mark Reviewed when it is due, and tap the quote to cycle Full, First Letters, and Hidden. Adding, editing, searching, and Settings are on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Import from URL

Paste a web page URL instead of typing: touch and hold + and choose Import from URL… on iPhone and iPad, or use the + menu or File → Import Quote… (⇧⌘I) on a Mac. Mezmorize downloads that one page and opens the New Quote form already filled in. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

  • Quote pages: fill the quote, author, and link (and the book when the page names one).
  • Scripture verses: link to a single verse or a range of verses. You get the verse text, the volume, and a citation such as 1 Nephi 3:7. The author is left blank. Whole chapters aren’t imported.
  • Hymns: fill all the verses, one line per row, the text author, and a title such as An Angel from on High (Hymns, no. 13).
  • Talks, magazine articles, and manuals: link to a single paragraph (use Copy Link or Share on the paragraph). You get that paragraph, the speaker as the author, the talk title, and a citation such as October 2026 general conference with the link. Whole talks aren’t imported.

Other pages are imported on a best-effort basis. Links that start with http are upgraded to https. If a site blocks the request, you are offline, or no quote is found, choose Enter Manually.

Sharing quotes

Export a set of quotes as a .mezmorize file: choose File → Export Quotes… (⇧⌘E) on a Mac, or the … menu, then Export Quotes…, on iPhone and iPad. To import one, open the file, or see Quotes. Nothing is added until you choose Import.

Reminders

Mezmorize can send one notification a day, only on days with quotes due or overdue. It shows how many quotes are due, with an optional quote preview you can turn off for privacy. The app icon badge shows the due count, and tapping a reminder opens Today’s Review.

Mezmorize asks for permission after you save your first quote that uses the memory method. To change the time, the preview, or turn reminders off, open Settings: choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,) on a Mac, or tap the … menu, then Settings, on iPhone and iPad.

Usage Data

Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts, such as how many quotes are reviewed. This is on by default. Quote text, authors, and web addresses are never sent. To turn it off, open Settings and turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data under Privacy: choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,) on a Mac, or tap the … menu, then Settings, on iPhone and iPad. See Privacy for details.
