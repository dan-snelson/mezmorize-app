Support

In-app Help

Mezmorize includes Help that explains the method and how to use the app.

  • Mac: choose Help → Mezmorize Help.
  • iPhone: tap the ? button on the tile screen.
  • iPad: tap the ? button in the list column.

Reminders

Mezmorize can send one notification a day, only on days with quotes due or overdue. It shows how many quotes are due, with an optional quote preview you can turn off for privacy. The app icon badge shows the due count, and tapping a reminder opens Today’s Review.

Mezmorize asks for permission after you save your first quote that uses the memory method. To change the time, the preview, or turn reminders off, open Settings: choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,) on a Mac, or tap the gear button on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question or idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post personal information in public issues.
