Support

In-app Help

Mezmorize includes Help that explains the method and how to use the app.

  • Mac: choose Help → Mezmorize Help.
  • iPhone and iPad: tap the … menu, then Help.

Reminders

Mezmorize can send one notification a day, only on days with quotes due or overdue. It shows how many quotes are due, with an optional quote preview you can turn off for privacy. The app icon badge shows the due count, and tapping a reminder opens Today’s Review.

Mezmorize asks for permission after you save your first quote that uses the memory method. To change the time, the preview, or turn reminders off, open Settings: choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,) on a Mac, or tap the … menu, then Settings, on iPhone and iPad.

Usage Data

Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts, such as how many quotes are reviewed. This is on by default. Quote text, authors, and web addresses are never sent. To turn it off, open Settings and turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data under Privacy: choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,) on a Mac, or tap the … menu, then Settings, on iPhone and iPad. See Privacy for details.
