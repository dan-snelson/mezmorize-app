Import a Quote from a Web Page

Bring in one quote at a time from a web page. On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold + and choose Import from URL…. On a Mac, use the + menu or File → Import Quote… (⇧⌘I). Mezmorize downloads that one page and opens the New Quote form already filled in. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

It works best with a link to a single quote or a single scripture verse (or a range of verses). A quote page fills the quote, author, and link, and the book when the page names one. A scripture verse fills the verse text, the volume, and a citation such as 1 Nephi 3:7, and leaves the author blank. Whole chapters aren’t imported. Other pages are imported on a best-effort basis.

If a site blocks the request, you are offline, or no quote is found, choose Enter Manually.

Quote Collections

These are ready-made quote collections. Download one, then open it in Mezmorize. On a Mac, choose File → Import Quotes from File… (⌥⌘I). On iPhone or iPad, open the file from Files, or choose Import from File… in the + menu. A preview lists every quote; untick any you don’t want, then choose Import.

Imported quotes are due for review the day you import them.
