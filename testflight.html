TestFlight

Join the beta

Try Mezmorize before it reaches the App Store. The beta is distributed through Apple’s TestFlight and is available for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Requirements

  • An Apple Account.
  • A Mac running macOS 27.2 or later, or an iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 27.2 or later.
  • The free TestFlight app (on Mac, from the Mac App Store; on iPhone or iPad, from the App Store).

How to join

  1. Install TestFlight (from the Mac App Store on Mac, or the App Store on iPhone or iPad).
  2. Open the public invite link on that device.
  3. Click or tap Accept, then Install (or Update).
  4. Future builds arrive through TestFlight, which notifies you.

On Mac, the installed beta app appears in Applications, and TestFlight manages updates.

Invite link

The public invite link is coming soon.

Send feedback

On iPhone or iPad, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. On Mac, choose Send Beta Feedback from the TestFlight app (or the app’s menu) instead of sharing a screenshot. Crash reports can also be sent through TestFlight prompts. You can also open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post personal information in public issues.

Beta notes

  • Builds expire after 90 days, so install updates when they arrive.
  • Data may be reset between beta builds.
  • iCloud sync is not available yet.

Usage Data

Anonymous usage counts are on by default and can be turned off in Settings. See Privacy for details.
