Transcript

Hi! This is Mezmorize, a free app for remembering the scriptures and Conference quotes you love. Sixty seconds. Here we go.

You mark a verse in Gospel Library, hear a line in Conference, and a week later it’s fading. Reading once isn’t enough.

Mezmorize uses the Three-minute Memory Method: five quick reviews over a year. Today, next week, next month, next quarter, next year. Total time is said to be about three minutes.

Adding is easy. In Gospel Library, tap Share on a verse, hymn, or Conference paragraph and choose Mezmorize. Check it, save it. Or import a ready-made set.

Each day, Today’s Review shows what’s due. Hide the words, or slide a virtual index card down one phrase at a time. Then tap Mark Reviewed.

Join the free TestFlight beta for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Your quotes sync privately through your iCloud, with no account. Scan, add a quote, and start remembering.

Requirements

  • An Apple Account.
  • A Mac running macOS 27.2 or later, or an iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 27.2 or later.
  • The free TestFlight app (on Mac, from the Mac App Store; on iPhone or iPad, from the App Store).

How to join

  1. Install TestFlight (from the Mac App Store on Mac, or the App Store on iPhone or iPad).
  2. Open the public invite link on that device.
  3. Click or tap Accept, then Install (or Update).
  4. Future builds arrive through TestFlight, which notifies you.

On Mac, the installed beta app appears in Applications, and TestFlight manages updates.

Send feedback

On iPhone or iPad, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. On Mac, choose Send Beta Feedback from the TestFlight app (or the app’s menu) instead of sharing a screenshot. Crash reports can also be sent through TestFlight prompts. You can also open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post personal information in public issues.

Beta notes

  • Builds expire after 90 days, so install updates when they arrive.
  • Data may be reset between beta builds.
  • The review-only Apple Watch companion is part of the iPhone beta and installs alongside the iPhone app.

Usage Data
