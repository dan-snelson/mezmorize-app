Privacy

Last updated: 19-Nov-2026

  • Your quotes and review data stay on your device, stored on-disk by the app.
  • Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts by default. You can turn this off in Settings. There is no tracking and no advertising.
  • There are no accounts to create.

Import Quote

When you choose Import Quote and enter a web address, Mezmorize fetches that single page, only when you ask. The page is used temporarily to fill the form. Nothing is saved unless you save the quote.

Reminders

Daily review reminders are notifications created on your device. No server is involved, and nothing about your quotes is sent anywhere.

Usage Data

Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. This is on by default.

What is sent: counts such as quotes added (typed or imported), reviews marked and which review stage they were, whether an import worked and from what kind of site, whether reminders are on or off and when a reminder is tapped, and a daily size range for your collection (for example, 6–20 quotes). It also includes standard app metadata: platform, OS and app version, device model, and locale.

The identifier: a random ID is created on your device. It is sent only as a hash and is not linked to you.

What is never sent: quote text, authors, sources, web addresses, search text, or anything you type.

To turn it off: open Settings, find Privacy, and turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data. On a Mac, choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,). On iPhone and iPad, tap the gear button. Mezmorize stops sending right away.

iCloud

iCloud sync is not currently active.
