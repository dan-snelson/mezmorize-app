Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Mezmorize is made by Dan K. Snelson. For privacy questions, email support@snelson.us.

The short version

  • Your quotes and review data stay on your devices and in your private iCloud account.
  • There are no accounts to create.
  • Your quotes are never sent to the developer or to analytics.
  • Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts by default. You can turn this off in Settings. There is no tracking and no advertising.
  • Mezmorize uses the network only to sync your quotes through your private iCloud account, when you import a quote from a web address, and, unless you turn it off, to send anonymous usage counts.

Import Quote

When you choose Import Quote and enter a web address, Mezmorize fetches that single page, only when you ask. The page is used temporarily to fill the form. Nothing is saved unless you save the quote.

Reminders

Daily review reminders are notifications created on your device. No server is involved, and nothing about your quotes is sent anywhere.

Usage Data

Mezmorize sends anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service that processes this data on our behalf. This is on by default.

What is sent: counts such as quotes added (typed or imported), reviews marked and which review stage they were, whether an import worked and from what kind of site, whether reminders are on or off and when a reminder is tapped, and a daily size range for your collection (for example, 6–20 quotes). It also includes standard app metadata: platform, OS and app version, device model, and locale.

The identifier: a hashed, app-specific device identifier. It is hashed on your device and again on TelemetryDeck’s servers, so neither TelemetryDeck nor the developer can recover the original identifier. It is not linked to you. In App Store privacy terms, Mezmorize collects Device ID and Product Interaction data, neither linked to you, used only for analytics and never for tracking.

What is never sent: quote text, authors, sources, web addresses, search text, or anything you type.

To turn it off: open Settings, find Privacy, and turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data. On a Mac, choose Mezmorize → Settings… (⌘,). On iPhone and iPad, tap the … menu, then Settings. Mezmorize stops sending right away.

Retention and deletion

TelemetryDeck stores anonymous usage data under its own retention terms, described in its privacy policy. Usage data is anonymous and cannot be tied to a person, so there is no personal record for the developer to look up or delete. You can stop collection at any time by turning off Share Anonymous Usage Data, and sending stops at once. If you have questions, email support@snelson.us.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion is review only. It gets your quotes through your private iCloud account, collects no analytics, and sends nothing else anywhere.

Files you share

Exporting quotes creates a small .mezmorize file on your device. It goes only where you send it, such as AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files. Importing a file reads it on your device.

iCloud

Mezmorize syncs your quotes and review data through your private iCloud account (Apple’s CloudKit), so they appear on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer cannot see it. If iCloud is not available, quotes stay on your device and sync when it is. Settings → iCloud shows whether iCloud is available.
